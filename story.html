Behind the Threads

Every Knot Carries a Hand, a Place, and a Story

Before a rug ever reaches a showroom floor, it has passed through the hands of spinners, dyers and weavers — often across many months, sometimes across generations. This is what stands behind everything we sell and everything we hold in the Collection.

Our Heritage

Three Generations in the Oriental Carpet Business

Established contacts, exclusive collections, and the top selection of rugs and kilim define us. Our unwavering passion for the industry, paired with meticulous attention to quality, fuels our inspiration. By importing directly into South Africa, we guarantee highly competitive prices, embodying our commitment to excellence.

01 · Sourcing

Direct Relationships, Not Middlemen

We work directly with weaving families and long-standing dealers across Persia, the Caucasus, and Central Asia. Every piece is inspected in person before it is offered for sale or admitted into the Collection.

02 · Craftsmanship

The Quality of the Knot

Knot density, wool quality, and the integrity of natural dyes are assessed on every rug that enters our care. Hand-knotted construction means no two pieces are ever truly identical — small irregularities are part of the hand that made it.

03 · Restoration & Care

Built to Outlive Us

Our in-house specialists clean, repair and conserve rugs old and new — from routine care for a new purchase to full restoration of a century-old antique bound for the Collection.

04 · Stewardship

Why Some Rugs Are Never Sold Online

Certain pieces — chosen for their craftsmanship, character or limited availability — are held back from our online range and kept in-store as part of our Collection. We believe pieces like these deserve to be seen, felt and discussed in person before they find their next home.
