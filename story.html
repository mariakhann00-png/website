Behind the Threads

A story woven across three generations.

Our story began in Kashmir, where our family traded handcrafted shawls and rugs across the region.

Over generations, that knowledge travelled with us — eventually bringing us to South Africa, where Oriental Carpet Gallery was established in 1998.

Kashmir → Lahore → London → Johannesburg → Cape Town

The Journey

01

Kashmir

Where it began

Our family’s story began in Kashmir, where handcrafted shawls and rugs were traded throughout the region.

02

Learning the Craft

Knowledge passed down

Over generations, knowledge of sourcing, restoration and retail was learned through experience and passed down through the family.

03

A Growing Legacy

Across generations

As the family expanded, so did the trade, with businesses established across Lahore, Singapore, London and beyond.

04

South Africa

1998

Recognising an opportunity to introduce a wider selection of authentic handmade rugs to South Africa, Oriental Carpet Gallery was established in Johannesburg and Cape Town.

05

Today

The tradition continues

After more than 25 years, we stand as one of South Africa’s leading names in fine rugs. Our collection is shaped by enduring relationships with artisans, suppliers and collectors around the world.

Three generations on,
the story continues.
