The Art of the Persian Rug

01 — The Thread

It begins with the finest materials, selected for their strength, softness and ability to develop character over time.

02 — The Hand

Each knot is placed by hand, slowly transforming individual threads into intricate patterns that can take months or even years to complete.

03 — The Pattern

Every motif carries a history. Colours, symbols and compositions passed from one generation to the next give each rug its distinctive character.

Behind the Threads

A Story Woven Across Generations

For generations, our family has travelled the world’s weaving regions in search of extraordinary handmade rugs. Every generation has added its own expertise, deepening a collection built on trust, craftsmanship and an enduring appreciation for the art of rug making. That tradition continues today with every rug we source and every client we serve.
